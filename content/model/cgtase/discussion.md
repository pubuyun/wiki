## Discussion

### Characterization

The characterize stage successfully identified CGTase as a functional starting enzyme for Cys-Gly-3M3SH chemical modification. The experimental assay showed activity of wild-type CGTase compared with the control, aligning with the characterization outcome, where docking and MD simulations support the feasibility of  glycosylation catalysed by CGTase. 

### Rational Design

Feasibility of the rational design, however, is not fully verified because of the limited dataset. Only T370R/F183W was successfully tested as designed. Comparing this double mutant with the unintended T370L variant provides limited evidence that the proposed design strategy deserves further evaluation.

### Limitation and Next Step

The intended T370K mutant was unexpectedly obtained as T370L. Therefore, no strong conclusion can yet be drawn about the effectiveness of rational design. Future work should test more mutants, especially T370K, T370R, and combination mutants, with more experimental replicates.
