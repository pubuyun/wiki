Inspired by [GB-SCIE 2025](https://2025.igem.wiki/greatbay-scie/), we set out to engineer an enzyme that can covalently modify the precursor. By attaching an additional group to the precursor before bacterial uptake, we aimed to [reduce its recognition and transport](/model/transporter-binder/characterize) :fn-ref{#1} by the transporter protein.
