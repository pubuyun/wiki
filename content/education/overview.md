## Target profile

We consider six dimensions when planning an education activity. Age, educational level, and audience size are fixed characteristics that we have limited influence over. Knowledge of synbio, interest in synbio, and engagement with synbio are dimensions that our activities can help develop.

Each dimension is scored from 1 to 5. The profile below describes an audience of public high school students.

::content-components-education-target-profile-radar
---
profile:
  Age: [1.8, "Teenagers are generally curious, receptive, and open-minded."]
  Educational level: [2.6, "Public high school students, mainly in Grades 10 and 11."]
  Size: [3.5, "Approximately 50 students."]
  Knowledge of synbio: [4.1, "Most students have studied genetic engineering and are familiar with basic synbio concepts, as these topics are frequently assessed in their biology curriculum. Some also participate in the National High School Biology Competition and possess more advanced biological knowledge."]
  Interest in synbio: [3.5, "Most students are interested in biology and eager to explore topics beyond the standard curriculum."]
  Engagement with synbio: [3.6, "Based on feedback from our previous activity, some students are considering studying synbio-related subjects at university or pursuing future careers in the field."]
---
::
