## Overview

Science moves forward when more people can take part. Our inclusion journey connects the voices we heard with the actions we took, guided by our **LIFT+** framework: Learn, Intervene, Feedback, Transfer, and Beyond.

:content-components-inclusivity-journey

We began with six target groups in China who often face barriers to accessing synthetic biology and broader science. Explore our contributions below.

## Blind Community

We created **20 tactile biology graphics** across five categories: cell biology, genetics and molecular biology, organ systems, organs, and specialized anatomical structures.

[Explore our tactile biology graphics and inclusion journey](/inclusivity/blind-community).

## Deaf Community

We produced **3 SignBio series videos** covering synthetic biology, cell structures, and molecules of life, **1 sign language version of our SynBio Song**, and **1 sign language game** on our wiki.

[Try our sign language game](/inclusivity/game).

## Autism Spectrum

For children on the autism spectrum, we developed **4 multi-sensory science teaching kits** covering plants, animals, bacteria, and the human body.

## Low Socio-economic Backgrounds

Our **Handy Lab experimental toolkit** features six experiments across three disciplines: physics (Floating Egg and Bending Water), chemistry (Red Cabbage pH Indicator and Mini “Volcano”), and biology (Strawberry DNA Extraction and Thirsty Celery).

## Modeling Beginners

We created **1 modeling guidebook** covering de novo binder design, molecular dynamics, and property prediction. Our **modeling software with a graphical user interface** supports three workflows: structure prediction, ligand binder design, and protein binder design.

[Explore our modeling workflows](/model) and [our software](/software).

## Older Adults

For older adults with limited formal education, we developed **1 community-based science engagement approach**. A hands-on sachet-making activity connected traditional crafts with synthetic biology, while science education posters communicated scientific knowledge and addressed common misconceptions about GM food.
