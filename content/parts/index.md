## Abstract

Our project explores multiple synthetic biology solutions to axillary odor by targeting the production of 3\-methyl\-3\-sulfanylhexan\-1\-ol \(3M3SH\), a key volatile compound associated with axillary odor, from the precursor,*S\-*\(1\-hydroxy\-3\-methylhexan\-3\-yl\)\-ʟ‑cysteinylglycine \(Cys\-Gly\-3M3SH\)\. To address this problem, we investigated three approaches, including AI\-assisted *de novo* designed precursor binders that reduces the availability of odor precursors,  *de novo* transporter binders that limit the transportation rate, and rationally engineered cyclodextrin glucanotransferase that glycosylate the precursor, reducing its recognition by the transporter PepTSh\. In parallel, we investigated the biosynthesis of terpenoid esters with woody and floral scents based on preferences expressed in our survey, as a complement to our odor\-management strategies\. As a result, we have characterized numerous parts shown below\.
