**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

When did you first notice that you had underarm odor?

**Mr. Chen**

When I was in high school.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Would you describe your underarm odor as mild, moderate, or severe?

**Mr. Chen**

Mild. The odor is not very strong.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Has underarm odor caused any difficulties in your daily life, such as with clothing, social interactions, work, or studies?

**Mr. Chen**

Not really, because my odor is not very strong.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What methods have you used to manage your underarm odor, and how effective were they?

**Mr. Chen**

I bought a liquid medication from a pharmacy in Henan Province and applied it to my underarms. After using it, I developed sores, broken skin, and severe itching. I thought I was allergic to it, so I stopped using it. Now I use a Japanese cream that your cousin gave me.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

How often do you use these deodorant products?

**Mr. Chen**

I used to apply a little of the liquid medication every day. Once I noticed the allergic reaction, I stopped using it. I have not experienced any side effects from the cream I use now, but I rarely use it because I hardly have any odor anymore.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What drawbacks have you noticed in the products you have used?

**Mr. Chen**

I am very satisfied with my current cream. The main problem with the previous liquid medication was its severe side effects. I cannot remember its price.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What improvements would you like to see in future solutions?

**Mr. Chen**

I think most topical products are not very effective, so the underlying cause may need to be addressed. Ideally, there could be an oral medication that works inside the body to kill the bacteria responsible for the odor. I feel that the products I have used only mask the odor rather than kill the bacteria, so they do not solve the underlying problem.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Have you ever considered surgery?

**Mr. Chen**

No, I have never considered it.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Having heard our introduction to the new product, what matters most to you?

**Mr. Chen**

If it can target those bacteria biologically and address the problem at its source, that would be ideal. Surgery always involves risks, such as wounds or lasting problems if the procedure is incomplete. I think your approach of intervening at the source is very good.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What other aspects of the product are important to you?

**Mr. Chen**

Effectiveness comes first, followed by having no side effects. Since it would be a product for long-term use, it should not be too expensive. A price similar to ordinary over-the-counter products would be reasonable.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

How long would you like a single application to last?

**Mr. Chen**

Three to four days, or a week.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What price range would you find most acceptable?

**Mr. Chen**

The product I buy now is not cheap either. It costs around RMB 50–60 for 20 mL. I think RMB 60 for 10–20 mL would be reasonable. If it works very well, people could accept RMB 80, RMB 90, or even RMB 100. A 20 mL product could last about two months when sprayed or applied to the skin.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

If the product proves effective, would you recommend it to friends with similar concerns?

**Mr. Chen**

Definitely.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

If the effectiveness were similar, would you prefer our protein-based product or existing antiperspirants and deodorant sprays? Why?

**Mr. Chen**

I would definitely choose your product, even if it were slightly more expensive. Many other products have side effects. With the liquid medication I used before, I had to spend money on hospital treatment because the side effects were so severe.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Do you have any other questions or suggestions about our product?

**Mr. Chen**

I would like the side effects to be minimal and the effect to last longer, so it would not need to be sprayed every day. For example, one application could last three to four days.
