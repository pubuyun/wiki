**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Which country or cultural environment did you mainly grow up in?

**Mr. Maz**

I was born and raised in the UK. The wider environment was British, but my household environment was Bangladeshi, a South Asian culture. So I grew up in two different cultural environments.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

In your cultural community, how is underarm odor generally perceived?

**Mr. Maz**

I believe most people would think similarly to me: no one wants to smell someone else's underarm odor. It is generally perceived negatively. You do not want to have noticeable odor yourself, and you do not want to smell it from other people either.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Do people with noticeable underarm odor experience stigma, social exclusion, or unfair treatment?

**Mr. Maz**

I think they probably do, even if people do not want to admit that they judge someone because of their odor.

If I am in a public place and someone else's underarm odor is very strong, it feels quite intrusive to my personal space, and I find it unpleasant. I imagine people with strong odor may therefore be treated somewhat differently. It may not be extreme, but others might keep their distance.

In more extreme cases, they could be bullied at school, especially at a young age. In the workplace, people might avoid them or have difficult conversations about it. It is a difficult factor to ignore, so I think there is some stigma.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What factors have shaped your perception of underarm odor? For example, family, friends, cultural norms, laws, media, advertising, or personal experience?

**Mr. Maz**

Mostly cultural norms. In the UK, people generally like to smell pleasant and think about how others perceive them. If you smell unpleasant, you may feel self-conscious and want to avoid that in public. That is one reason I use deodorant.

Media also play a role. Growing up, we saw advertisements for deodorants, perfumes, and colognes. These are all promoted as ways to smell nicer. From what I see, most people use some kind of product for that purpose.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Have your views on underarm odor changed over time? What influenced that change?

**Mr. Maz**

Definitely. When you are younger, you think more about how you smell.

**Mr. Maz**

One factor I look for in a deodorant is that it does not stain my clothes. Previously, I noticed brown, orange, or yellowish stains on white T-shirts and other clothing. I understand that some staining comes from sweat itself, but I still do not want a product that stains clothes.

The second factor is how long it remains effective throughout the day. Products often claim 12 or 24 hours of protection, which is usually enough. You have to experiment a little because some are not as effective as advertised. I look for products that work better for longer.

Interestingly, fragrance is not an important factor for me. Most products smell pleasant enough, so I do not particularly focus on the scent.

The third factor is whether the product reduces how much I sweat during the day. I understand that during intense exercise, my body needs to sweat, so the product may not work as well. For everyday use, though, reducing sweat is useful.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

You have already mentioned some of the things you like and dislike about deodorants. What do you think could be improved?

**Mr. Maz**

What I like about my current product is that it claims not to stain clothes, and that seems to be the case. Some products I used previously left a white streak when the roll-on touched my clothing.

I think deodorants could be stronger overall. They work adequately at first, but their effectiveness could be improved.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Do you mean the effect should last longer and provide better control of sweat odor?

**Mr. Maz**

Yes. I notice my own sweat odor more than other people do. You may not be able to smell it, but I can.

If the product worked more strongly, I would feel more confident that I did not smell unpleasant as the day went on. It works at the beginning, but as sweat builds up throughout the day, I feel that I start to smell worse. I would like stronger protection.
