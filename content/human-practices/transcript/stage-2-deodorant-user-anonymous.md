**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

We would now like to introduce our proposed solution. We found that odor is produced when skin surface Staphylococcus bacteria convert odorless precursor substances secreted by apocrine glands into smelly compounds. Our product is not a sweat blocker or antibacterial agent. Instead, using synthetic biology, we design a protein that binds to odor precursor molecules in the armpit, preventing bacteria from breaking them down, thus stopping odor formation. We are considering making it into a spray or roll-on, but usage frequency is not yet determined. After hearing this, do you have any concerns or think there might be problems?

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**
