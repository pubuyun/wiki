**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Hello. We are students from Shenzhen College of International Education participating in iGEM, the International Genetically Engineered Machine competition. We hope to use synthetic biology to develop a more comfortable and environmentally friendly underarm deodorant. Your feedback will help us improve our project. Thank you for your support.

When did you first become aware of underarm odor?

**Mr. Wu**

When I was in high school, a roommate had strong body odor that affected everyone around him. That was when I started paying attention to the issue.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Would you describe your own underarm odor as mild, moderate, or severe?

**Mr. Wu**

When I was younger, I hardly had any odor, so I would describe it as mild. It has become stronger as I have grown older, and I would now consider it moderate.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What difficulties has underarm odor caused in your daily life, such as in social situations, relationships, work, studies, or clothing choices?

**Mr. Wu**

In summer, when I sweat more, I worry about bothering other people, especially when sharing a car.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What methods have you used to manage underarm odor, and how effective have they been?

**Mr. Wu**

I bought Xishilan from a pharmacy. It works well.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What do you currently use, and how often?

**Mr. Wu**

In summer, I spray Xishilan on my underarms once or twice a month.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

How quickly does it work, how long does the effect last, and how convenient is it to use?

**Mr. Wu**

It works quickly, and the effect lasts around ten days or more. It is a spray, so it is convenient to use.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Have you experienced any side effects?

**Mr. Wu**

It makes my skin rough.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What do you think of its scent and price?

**Mr. Wu**

It has almost no scent. I have not paid much attention to the price, but it is within an acceptable range.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Have you considered surgery?

**Mr. Wu**

No, I have not considered it.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What improvements would you like to see in future products?

**Mr. Wu**

I would like something gentler on the skin, with fewer side effects. Many products on the market use chemical agents, and I feel they may damage the skin while performing their intended function. I am not sure whether they stop the skin from secreting something, but after spraying them, my skin feels rough, almost like toad skin. That is why I feel these products can have significant side effects.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Research indicates that underarm odor can arise when a type of Staphylococcus on the skin converts an odorless precursor secreted by the apocrine glands into an odorous substance.

Our proposed product would neither stop sweating nor kill bacteria. Instead, we are using synthetic biology to design a protein that binds to the odor precursor, preventing Staphylococcus from breaking it down and releasing the odor. It could be developed as a spray or a roll-on. We have not yet determined the application frequency and would like to understand your preferences to guide development.

Having heard this description, what matters most to you?

**Mr. Wu**

I care about how long the effect lasts and whether the product is natural and safe. I would expect a product made using synthetic biology to be gentler on the skin than chemical products.

A spray would be convenient. With a roll-on, I would worry that contact with the skin could contaminate the product inside the bottle. A price below RMB 100 would be acceptable. I am also interested in when your product might become available.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

If the effectiveness were similar, would you prefer our protein-based product or existing antiperspirants and deodorant sprays? Why?

**Mr. Wu**

I would definitely prefer the protein-based product. I think it would be a better option.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What price range would you find most acceptable?

**Mr. Wu**

Less than RMB 100 per bottle, with each bottle lasting around three months—roughly RMB 1 per use.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

If the product proves effective, would you recommend it to friends with similar concerns?

**Mr. Wu**

Yes, I would be happy to.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Do you have any other questions or suggestions about our product?

**Mr. Wu**

If it works well, I would look forward to being able to buy it.

My understanding is that your approach uses synthetic biology, enzymes, or something like a false key occupying the original keyhole. I think this kind of biological blocking approach could be gentler and have very little adverse impact on people. The challenge is whether it can be as effective as chemical products.

Skin appearance matters, especially to women who care about how their skin looks. If a product leaves the skin rough like toad skin, people will not want to use it. I think synthetic biology is a promising approach to addressing body odor.

I see several key challenges. First, can the product achieve the expected effectiveness? Second, can the effect last longer so that it needs to be applied less often? Third, how long would its shelf life be—one, two, three, or four years? How could the other ingredients help preserve it for longer?

Cost is also important. Could it stay below RMB 100 per bottle and last around three months? Those are the most important considerations for me.
