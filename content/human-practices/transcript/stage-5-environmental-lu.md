for both plastic packaging and aluminum packaging, the environmental impacts are mainly concentrated in four stages. For plastics, as a petroleum-based material, they mainly come from crude oil. Plastics originate from crude oil, whether from domestic sources or from the Middle East, the US, or Russia.
