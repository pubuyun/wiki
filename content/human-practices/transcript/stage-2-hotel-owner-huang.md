**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Do hotels pay particular attention to body odor as part of their staff grooming and hygiene standards?

**Mr. Huang**

Yes. Other service businesses, such as restaurants, also pay attention to this.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

In your experience, have guests complained about strong underarm odor from staff?

**Mr. Huang**

Guests rarely make formal complaints about this. Most complaints concern hotel cleanliness and facilities. Guests may feel uncomfortable about body odor without raising the issue directly.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

How would a hotel address this issue?

**Mr. Huang**

Hotels generally require all staff, especially front-desk staff, to maintain good personal hygiene. Supervisors will remind staff when necessary.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Do many hotel employees use deodorants or antiperspirants?

**Mr. Huang**

I have not looked into this specifically. I have not noticed unpleasant body odor from staff in chain hotels, possibly because of their hygiene standards.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Could hotels purchase gentle underarm care products for staff who need them?

**Mr. Huang**

Providing them to every employee may not be appropriate, since not everyone is concerned about body odor, and there would also be a cost. It would be better to allow interested staff to purchase the products themselves through the hotel's supply chain.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Could underarm deodorant become a complimentary hotel room amenity, like toothbrushes or shampoo?

**Mr. Huang**

Cost is very important. In mid-range hotels, the total cost of disposable amenities per room is less than RMB 20. If the deodorant is inexpensive enough, it could be offered as an additional amenity in selected rooms.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Do guests need deodorant, for example before business meetings or after exercise?

**Mr. Huang**

Yes, some guests do. Some currently use perfume instead. The cost could be included in the room rate, or small packs could be sold in the hotel lobby, rather than making it a standard item in every room.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Since deodorant is a personal product, could offering it make guests feel embarrassed?

**Mr. Huang**

Yes. Rather than selling or handing it to guests at the front desk, we could place it in their rooms and let them decide whether to use it. This would help avoid embarrassment.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Which would work better: placing it in every room or allowing guests to request it through the hotel's app?

**Mr. Huang**

Both options could work. Cost remains the main consideration. Some hotel brands already provide additional small personal care products in their rooms.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

Should the product come in a small, single-use format for hotels?

**Mr. Huang**

Yes, that is very important. Small sample sizes, such as 3–5 mL, would allow guests to try it while keeping costs down. If they like it, they may ask to purchase a full-size version.

**GreatBay-SCIE 2026**

What price per sample would be acceptable to hotels?

**Mr. Huang**

Pricing would be negotiated by the hotel's supply chain team. The product would also need to pass safety checks. I am not sure about the specific commercial pricing details.
