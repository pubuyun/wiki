#left
## Strengths

- Targets 3M3SH production rather than masking odor.
- Preserves axillary microbiome balance.
- Gently controls odor without blocking sweat.
- Rational design based on the 3M3SH mechanism.
- Multiple solutions can be optimized independently.
- Uses synthetic biology and precision targeting.
- Suitable for adolescents and pregnant women.
- Considers mental well-being and reduces stigma.
- Adaptive packaging improves accessibility.

#right
## Weaknesses

- Binding capacity may become saturated.
- Biological ingredients may cost more.
- Performance varies between individuals.
- Limited human-skin validation.
- Effects may be less immediately noticeable.

#left-bottom
## Opportunities

- Growing demand for microbiome-friendly personal care.
- Consumer dissatisfaction with conventional deodorants.
- Some deodorants lack strong scientific foundations.
- Advances in microbiome research and synthetic biology.
- Growing biotechnology interest in personal care.
- Increasing focus on transparency, skin health, and sustainability.
- Large and diverse potential customer base.
- Expanding synthetic biology market.

#right-bottom
## Threats

- Conventional deodorants already satisfy many consumers.
- Established brands have strong market advantages.
- Consumer hesitation toward biotechnology.
- Public misunderstanding may affect acceptance.
- Individual microbiome differences may affect consistency.
- Different regulations may increase costs and approval time.
