### Key Partners

- **Link Spider:** Lab provision, mentorship, and mass-production agency.
- **GenScript and CWBiotech:** Parts manufacturing.
- **KleanK:** Deodorant-industry advice and outreach opportunities.

### Key Activities and Resources

Activities include background scientific research, protein development through modeling, product development including product formats and licensing, packaging research and design, and marketing and promotion. Resources include relationships with specialists and potential customers and laboratory support for binder and protein development.

### Value Propositions

1. Preventing underarm odor at its source by targeting the pathway forming 3M3SH rather than masking odor, broadly eliminating skin bacteria, or removing axillary apocrine glands.
2. Providing skin-friendly odor control for adolescents and adults during puberty, active lifestyles, and daily social interactions.
3. Maintaining axillary microbiome balance by selectively interfering with odor formation in *Staphylococcus hominis*.
4. Utilizing biosynthetic production methods to target the recently elucidated pathway, which, to our knowledge, existing deodorant products do not specifically target.
5. Offering adaptive packaging with force-assistive mechanics for users with physical disabilities.

### Customer Relationships

Personalized direct engagement provides tailored access and experiences through retail and e-commerce. Partner-enabled accessibility provides convenient, comfortable access through venue partnerships and self-service points.

### Customer Segments

- **Primary customers:** Individuals with conventional deodorant sensitivities or allergies, individuals unable or unsuitable to undergo axillary osmidrosis surgery, and users prioritizing underarm skin health and aesthetics.
- **Secondary end-users:** Beauty and personal-care practitioners and visitors; sports-fitness staff and members; travel passengers and transport crews; retail employees and shoppers; corporate office employees; and hospitality staff and guests.
- **Secondary customers:** *B2B* organizations owning and operating venues where secondary end-users are present.
- **Tertiary customers:** Anyone concerned about axillary odor.

### Distribution Channels

Direct selling (*B2C*) includes an official online store, social-media shops such as Rednote and TikTok, and pop-up stalls. Indirect selling (B2B) includes offline retailers such as drugstores, supermarkets, and convenience stores; live-streaming sales agencies; and venue-based wholesale partners such as gyms, hotels, and airlines.

### Cost Structures

Fixed costs cover laboratory equipment, lab rent, licensing, experimental consumables for protein and terpene ester development, and research and development. Variable costs cover ingredients (binder proteins, terpene esters, preservatives, and stabilizers), packaging, advertising and marketing, and logistics.

### Revenue Streams

- Retail price per unit: CNY 48 (around USD 7.15).
- Wholesale price per unit: CNY 35 (around USD 5.21).
