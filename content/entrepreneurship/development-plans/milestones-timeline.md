- **2026:** Market research and strategy planning, followed by research and development. Safety testing begins during product development; durability and packaging testing extend into 2027.
- **2027:** Effectiveness validation continues, followed by pilot preparation through profiling, advertising, and packaging. E-commerce launch precedes retailer testing and launch.
- **2028:** Production scale-up, risk surveillance and mitigation, long-term partnership formation, and sponsored research agreements.
