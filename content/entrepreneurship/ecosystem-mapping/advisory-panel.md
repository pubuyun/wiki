## Angelos Melatos

::

We invited Mr. Melatos to review our business plan from a business mentor’s perspective. Particularly, we focused our discussion on customer analysis, 2-year cash flow projections, and industry analysis. His feedback helped us evaluate the feasibility of our proposed business model and identify areas where additional industrial and financial expertise would be required. For example, he recommended that we add a written statement about our methodology for calculating *TAM*, *SAM*, and *SOM*, with clear references to industrial assumptions and supporting statistics.

## Lu Haoran

We consulted Mr. Lu regarding the industrialization of our proposed product. Our discussion focused on manufacturing scalability and production costs. It helped us distinguish between lab-based proof-of-concept results and the practical constraints of mass production, including cost boundaries and process stability. Mr. Lu also encouraged us to interview a cosmetics company specialising in protein-related products to draw on their peer-industry experience.

Dr. Wang reviewed our two-year cash flow projections and the assumptions behind our expected revenue, operating expenses, and equity financing. He advised us to assess whether our projected growth and cost structure were realistic for a biotechnology-based personal care startup. He also highlighted the importance of patent protection and answered our questions about the patent application process.

## Han Zhifei

Han provided constructive advice on the structure of our wiki presentation of Entrepreneurship. He also gave us guidance on building connections between our iHP engagement and Entrepreneurship, particularly for marketing and unmet needs identification, which are closely interconnected with our iHP engagement.

## Su Rongsheng

We shared our product design with Mr. Su, who highlighted key considerations for managing long-term negative impacts, particularly consumer perception, product safety, and formulation stability.
