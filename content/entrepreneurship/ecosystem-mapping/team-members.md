## Skills and department allocation

At the beginning of the project, we established five departments — Sales & Marketing, Product Development, Human Resources, Finance, and Facilities & Procurement — to clarify the capabilities required to support different aspects of our project. We then surveyed and assessed each member's existing skills, matching members to departments where their strengths could be best utilized.

## Skill gap analysis

After gathering information on each member’s skills, we conducted a holistic skill gap analysis using headcount to identify areas where our current team capacity may be insufficient relative to our organizational needs.

This analysis provides direction for our future hiring plan, but does not necessarily determine it. We understand the limitations of using headcount to represent team capabilities, as members’ skills may develop over time through project experience and collaboration.
