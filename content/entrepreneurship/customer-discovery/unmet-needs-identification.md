Through extensive iHP outreach, including customer interviews, surveys and expert consultation, we identified the following unmet needs in existing deodorant products.

## Reliable odor control

The teenage potential customer had tried several deodorants without satisfactory results, highlighting the need for more dependable effectiveness.

## Lower irritation and allergy risks

From Mr. Chen, who reported sores and an allergic reaction requiring medical treatment, and survey respondents who described physical discomfort.

## Affordability, particularly for adolescents

From the teenage interviewee, who preferred RMB 20–30, while adult interviewees generally accepted prices below RMB 100 if effectiveness was satisfactory.

## Suitable fragrance options

From Ms. Wu, who valued a pleasant scent, and local and international surveys showing different fragrance preferences.

## Convenient formats and a comfortable skin feel

From users expressing different preferences for roll-ons, sprays and creams, and Mr. Su’s advice to avoid complicated preparation and sticky formulations.

## Minimal clothing stains and residue

From international potential customer Mr. Islam and online product reviews examined early in the project, which highlighted concerns about clothing stains and residue.

## Fast, hygienic odor management

From the gym owner, who prioritized quick action and convenient shared use, and the hotel manager, who preferred affordable, replaceable single-use products.

## Accessible packaging

From inclusive-packaging research prompted by Ms. Troy’s comments: conventional caps and applicators may be difficult to use for older adults, people with limited hand mobility and people with visual impairments.

## More sustainable packaging

From Mr. Wood, who specifically valued environmentally friendly features such as refillable packaging.
